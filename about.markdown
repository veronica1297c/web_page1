Our laboratory is dedicated to understanding how complex biological systems respond to environmental stressors at the molecular, cellular, and organismal levels. We combine experimental biology with computational and omics-based approaches to address fundamental questions in genome stability, cellular adaptation, and human health.

Our work is motivated by the idea that biological responses are rarely driven by a single factor. Instead, they emerge from the interaction of multiple stressors, signaling pathways, and regulatory mechanisms. By studying these interactions, we aim to generate knowledge with both fundamental and translational relevance.

## Research Vision

We are particularly interested in how cells maintain genome integrity under extreme or non-physiological conditions, and how failures in these mechanisms contribute to long-term health risks. Our research integrates:

- Molecular and cell biology  
- Genomics, transcriptomics, and other omics approaches  
- Quantitative data analysis and bioinformatics  
- Experimental models relevant to human health  

By bridging basic science and applied research, we strive to connect molecular mechanisms with real-world biological outcomes.

---

## Our Approach

We believe that interdisciplinary research is essential to address complex biological problems. Our lab fosters close collaboration between experimentalists and computational scientists, allowing us to move seamlessly from data generation to biological interpretation.

We also value reproducibility, open science, and thoughtful experimental design. Whenever possible, our datasets, analysis pipelines, and methodologies are shared to promote transparency and collaboration within the scientific community.

## Training & Lab Culture

Training and mentorship are central to our mission. We aim to provide a supportive and intellectually stimulating environment for students and researchers at all stages of their careers. Lab members are encouraged to develop independence, critical thinking skills, and a strong scientific identity.

We value curiosity, collaboration, and respect, and we believe that good science is done best in an environment where people feel motivated, supported, and challenged.

---

## Collaborations & Impact

Our lab actively collaborates with national and international research groups across disciplines. Through these partnerships, we seek to expand the scope and impact of our work and contribute to a broader understanding of human biology in health and disease.

Ultimately, our goal is to generate knowledge that not only advances science, but also informs strategies to protect and improve human health.
